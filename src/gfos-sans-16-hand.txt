# GFOS Sans Regular 16 px: the characters drawn by hand at 16 px. Every other character is made from the 13 px
# face by gridfont.py grow (rows and a column added beside like ones), or, for Latin-1's accented letters, from the
# 16 px letters (compose). These are the ones grow can't make (no two like rows to grow by), and the figures
# 2 and 4 so that all figures are 8 wide.
# Rows 0-15 per glyph: ascent 13 (baseline under row 12), descent 3. Row 0 clear (marks), ascenders rows 1-12,
# capitals and figures rows 2-12 (11 px), x-height rows 5-12 (8 px), descenders rows 13-15.
# Same rules as 13 px: 1 px stems, no 2x2 blobs, one-pixel diagonal steps.
